# Code of Conduct

Bastión VPN contributors, maintainers, and participants are expected to keep technical collaboration respectful, specific, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent while being precise about impact and evidence.
- Welcome different experience levels and explain operational context.
- Give actionable review feedback focused on the change, not the person.
- Protect customer, plant, and credential information.
- Pause a discussion when a mistake could create a security or availability risk.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, credential sharing, deliberate exposure of customer topology, and knowingly unsafe operational instructions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub contact or security channel. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or close contributions when necessary to protect people or infrastructure.
